### 💻 Quick Rundown

I take software apart, figure out how it ticks, and then build clean stuff right on top of what I learn. From standalone desktop utilities to custom web ecosystems—*that's my lane.* 🛠️

- 🎯 **What I Do:** Reverse engineering applications, writing desktop tools (auto updates, configurations), designing full-stack sites (dashboards, keys), and dropping small scripts.
- 🛠️ **Main Project:** **R4L** — My primary desktop application and custom platform ecosystem.
- 📈 **Side Interest:** Lowkey tracking futures and memecoins when I'm away from the keyboard.
- 🔗 **Full Body of Work:** You can check out my live project dumps over at [r4lzy.github.io](https://github.io).
